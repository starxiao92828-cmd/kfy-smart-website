E4 B2B Buyer Questions

What type of B2B program is E4 designed for?

E4 is a flagship adjustable-bed-base platform for premium mattress, furniture and distribution programs requiring its sliding structure and current OKIN motor configuration.

What are the typical MOQ and lead time?

The current OKIN configuration typically starts from 50 pieces, with production lead time around 45 days. Final terms depend on quantity and specification.

What can be customized for E4 OEM/ODM programs?

Depending on project scope, E4 programs may include size, structure and finish, controls, functions, packaging and private-label coordination.

What should I provide before quotation?

Share destination market, size, quantity, motor/control requirements, furniture or mattress integration needs, branding and packaging requirements.
