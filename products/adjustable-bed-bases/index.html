Adjustable Bed Base FAQ

What is the standard MOQ for adjustable bed bases?

Standard programs generally start from 20 pieces. Programs using specific components or deeper customization may require higher minimums.

What is the typical lead time?

Typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to quantity, configuration and customization requirements.

Can KFY SMART support OKIN motor configurations?

Yes. Selected adjustable-bed programs can be configured with OKIN motors. These programs typically start from 50 pieces, with final requirements confirmed by model and project.

What can be customized for OEM/ODM programs?

Depending on project scope, options may include logo and branding, packaging, upholstery and colors, dimensions, motors/controllers, function combinations, structural development, tooling and appearance.

How are compliance documents handled?

Documentation is confirmed according to the selected product, components and destination market rather than applied universally across every configuration.

Can you estimate container loading before an order?

Yes. Approximate loading quantities can be calculated using the selected model, package dimensions and container type. Final quantities depend on the confirmed packaging configuration.
