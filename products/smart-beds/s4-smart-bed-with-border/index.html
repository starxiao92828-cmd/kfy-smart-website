S4 B2B Buyer Questions

What type of B2B program is S4 designed for?

S4 is a Flagship upholstered Smart Bed for premium furniture, hospitality and flagship retail projects requiring a complete upholstered-border presentation.

How does S4 differ from S3?

S4 uses a full upholstered-border furniture format, while S3 uses a refined open-frame upholstered platform. Final product selection depends on the intended furniture presentation and project requirements.

What are the typical MOQ and lead time?

Standard Smart Bed programs may start from 20 pieces, while OKIN configurations typically start from 50 pieces. Typical lead time is around 45 days. Final terms depend on quantity and configuration.

Are LED, massage, USB and Bluetooth standard on S4?

No. These options depend on the approved S4 configuration and are confirmed during project specification.
