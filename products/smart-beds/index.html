What Makes Up a Smart Bed Program?

A finished Smart Bed combines more than adjustable motion. The complete product program may include bed structure, upholstery, motion hardware, controls and market-specific OEM/ODM configuration.

01

Bed Structure

Iron-mesh support, wooden bed-board construction, refined open-frame platforms or full-border structures depending on model.

02

Motion System

Adjustable head and foot sections with motor and control configurations confirmed according to the selected product program.

03

Upholstery & Appearance

Fabric, texture, color, border treatment and finished-bed presentation can vary by model and project.

04

Comfort & Control Options

Depending on configuration, selected programs may include functions such as massage, remote controls, USB, LED lighting, Bluetooth or related control options.

05

OEM/ODM Integration

Branding, packaging, dimensions, function combinations, structure development, tooling and private-label coordination may be supported according to project scope.

Smart Bed Program FAQ

What is the difference between a Smart Bed and an Adjustable Bed Base?

An Adjustable Bed Base is primarily an adjustable motion platform. A Smart Bed program combines the finished bed structure and upholstery with adjustable motion, controls and configuration-dependent comfort or connectivity features.

What is the standard MOQ for Smart Bed programs?

Standard programs generally start from 20 pieces. Programs using specific components or deeper customization may require higher minimum quantities.

Can Smart Bed programs use OKIN motors?

Selected Smart Bed programs can be configured with OKIN motors. These programs typically start from 50 pieces, with final requirements confirmed by model and project.

What can be customized for OEM/ODM Smart Bed projects?

Depending on project scope, customization may include branding, packaging, upholstery and colors, dimensions, motors/controllers, function combinations, structural development, tooling and appearance.

What is the typical lead time?

Typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to quantity, selected configuration and customization requirements.

How do I choose between S1, S2, S3 and S4?

The range progresses from the value-focused S1 platform through mid-range S2 and high-end S3 to flagship S4. The appropriate model depends on target market, furniture presentation, feature requirements, budget and customization depth. S1-G is available as a specialized S1-family structural variant.
