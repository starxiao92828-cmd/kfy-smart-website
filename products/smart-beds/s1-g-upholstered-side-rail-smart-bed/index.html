S1-G B2B Buyer Questions

Is S1-G a separate Smart Bed core level?

No. S1-G is a specialized structural variant within the S1 family.

What makes S1-G different from S1?

S1-G uses an integrated upholstered side-rail structure, while the core S1 model uses an internal iron-mesh support format.

Is 90 × 195 cm the only available size?

No. It is the current reference configuration. Final sizes are confirmed according to project requirements.

What should buyers provide before quotation?

Share target size, quantity, upholstery, side-rail requirements, control configuration, branding and packaging expectations.
