S1 B2B Buyer Questions

What type of program is S1 designed for?

S1 is a Budget Smart Bed for entry-level B2B, distribution and private-label programs requiring a finished upholstered format with internal iron-mesh support.

Is the iron mesh the sleeping surface?

No. The iron mesh is an internal support structure within the finished upholstered Smart Bed.

What are the typical MOQ and lead time?

Standard Smart Bed programs may start from 20 pieces, with typical lead time around 45 days. Final terms depend on configuration and quantity.

What should buyers provide before quotation?

Share market, size, quantity, upholstery, motor/control requirements, branding and packaging expectations.
