W4 B2B Buyer Questions

How is W4 different from W1-W3 Smart Mattresses?

W1-W3 are mattress-led Smart Mattress platforms. W4 is an integrated system combining the smart mattress, upholstered bed body, removable side armrests and whole-bed lift.

What are the typical MOQ and lead time?

W4 MOQ is confirmed by project scope. Typical lead time is around 45 days, subject to the approved integrated configuration and quantity.

What warranty applies to W4?

The mattress warranty is typically 10 years. Motor, lift, electrical and other component warranties are confirmed separately during quotation.

What should buyers provide before quotation?

Share destination market, size, quantity, mattress requirements, upholstery, armrest configuration, electrical requirements, branding and packaging expectations.
