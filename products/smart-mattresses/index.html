How Smart Mattress Programs Differ

Smart mattress programs can differ in mattress construction, motion functions, control options and overall product format. The appropriate configuration depends on target-market positioning and project requirements.

01

Foam structure, mattress profile, surface materials and finished presentation vary by model.

02

Motion & Positioning

Selected models support configurable head and foot adjustment, preset positions or synchronized movement depending on product level.

03

Massage & Comfort Functions

Massage functions are model-specific. W2 and W3 include confirmed three-level vibration massage, while function combinations vary across the collection.

04

Control Interface

Remote and selected App/control options depend on the chosen model and configuration.

05

Product Format

W1-W3 are mattress-led smart platforms. W4 moves into an integrated system combining the smart mattress, upholstered bed body and removable side armrests.

Smart Mattress Program FAQ

What is the difference between W1, W2, W3 and W4?

The range progresses from the essential W1 platform to functional W2 and premium W3, while W4 is an integrated system combining the smart mattress with an upholstered bed body and removable side armrests.

What is the standard MOQ for Smart Mattress programs?

Standard programs generally start from 20 pieces. Deeper customization or specific project requirements may require different minimum quantities.

What is the typical production lead time?

Typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to quantity, selected model and customization requirements.

What is the mattress warranty?

Mattress warranty is typically 10 years. Final warranty scope and component-specific terms are confirmed according to the selected product and quotation.

What can be customized for OEM/ODM Smart Mattress programs?

Depending on the model and project scope, options may include size, surface fabrics, colors, materials configuration, controls, function combinations, electrical configuration, packaging and private-label details.

How should B2B buyers choose between W1-W3 and W4?

W1-W3 are mattress-led product platforms with increasing levels of functionality and positioning. W4 is designed as a more integrated system. The appropriate choice depends on target market, product positioning, feature requirements, distribution model and project scope.
