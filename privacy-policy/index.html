Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026
Last Updated: July 23, 2026

1. Introduction

KFY SMART respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website.

KFY SMART is operated by:

Anhui Care For You Network Technology Co., Ltd.

Room 407, 4th Floor, Building B, Phase III (South Zone), Independent Innovation Industrial Base, Huguang Road, Shushan Economic Development Zone, Shushan District, Hefei, Anhui, China.

Anhui Care For You Network Technology Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of KFY Group and operates the KFY SMART brand.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how and why we use it, who may process it, how long we retain it, and the rights that may be available to you.

2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • the KFY SMART website;
  • the Contact Us inquiry form;
  • communications resulting from a website inquiry;
  • website security, hosting and technical logs.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites or services linked from our website, such as LinkedIn or WhatsApp. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

When you submit an inquiry through our website, we may collect:

  • your name;
  • business email address;
  • company name;
  • country or region;
  • product category of interest;
  • specific product or model of interest;
  • estimated purchase quantity;
  • telephone or WhatsApp number, if voluntarily provided;
  • the content of your message or project requirements;
  • any other information you voluntarily include in your inquiry.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, identification documents, medical information or other sensitive personal information through the inquiry form.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access our website or submit an inquiry, limited technical or source information may be processed automatically, including:

  • the page from which the inquiry was submitted;
  • the page URL;
  • the product model associated with the inquiry;
  • referral source;
  • UTM source, medium or campaign information;
  • submission date and time;
  • IP address;
  • browser type;
  • device type and operating-system information;
  • server, security and error logs;
  • anti-bot verification results.

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to business inquiries and protect the website against spam, fraud, misuse and malicious activity.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • receive, review and respond to your inquiry;
  • understand your product, sourcing, quotation, sample or cooperation requirements;
  • communicate with you about the inquiry you submitted;
  • provide relevant product information, quotations or project responses;
  • assign your inquiry to an authorized member of our business team;
  • maintain reasonable records of business communications;
  • protect the website and inquiry form against spam, fraud and malicious activity;
  • diagnose technical or delivery problems;
  • comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not currently use website inquiry information to send newsletters, mass-marketing emails or unrelated promotional messages.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by applicable data-protection law, we may process personal information on one or more of the following grounds:

  • taking steps at your request before entering into a contract or business relationship;
  • our legitimate interests in responding to business inquiries, managing business communications and protecting website security;
  • compliance with applicable legal obligations;
  • your consent, where consent is specifically requested and legally required.

Submitting a business inquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing communications.

6. How Website Inquiries Are Handled

Website inquiries are delivered by email to authorized members of our business team. The email address entered by the customer is used as the Reply-To address so that our authorized team members can respond directly to the inquiry.

At this stage:

  • website inquiries are not automatically or manually entered into a CRM system;
  • inquiry information is accessible only to authorized personnel who require it for business follow-up;
  • inquiry information is not provided to cooperation factories;
  • inquiry information is not used for automated customer scoring;
  • inquiry information is not used to make decisions based solely on automated processing.

7. Service Providers

We use selected service providers to operate the website. We also plan to use selected service providers to protect and deliver the Contact Us inquiry form when that form is enabled.

7.1 Vercel

Our website is hosted and deployed using Vercel. Vercel may process website traffic, IP addresses, system configuration information, technical logs and other information necessary to provide website hosting, deployment, security and infrastructure services.

7.2 Resend

When the Contact Us inquiry form is enabled, we plan to use Resend to transmit website inquiry emails to authorized business email recipients. Information contained in an inquiry email may therefore be processed through Resend's email-delivery infrastructure. The customer email address will be used as the Reply-To address and will not be used as the technical sending address.

7.3 Cloudflare Turnstile

When the Contact Us inquiry form is enabled, we plan to use Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish legitimate visitors from automated bots and protect the inquiry form against spam and malicious submissions. Turnstile may process limited technical signals, such as an IP address, browser user-agent information, TLS characteristics, the website origin and the Turnstile site key, for bot-detection and website-security purposes.

7.4 Enterprise Email Services

Inquiry emails may also be processed and stored by the enterprise email systems used by KFY SMART and KFY Group.

We expect service providers to process information only for the services they provide and in accordance with their contractual, privacy and security obligations.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

We do not:

  • sell, rent, exchange or trade customer inquiry information;
  • upload customer inquiry lists to advertising platforms;
  • use inquiry information for advertising retargeting;
  • provide inquiry information to cooperation factories;
  • provide inquiry information to unrelated third parties.

We may share or disclose information only:

  • to authorized KFY SMART or KFY Group personnel who need it to respond to the inquiry;
  • to service providers supporting website hosting, email delivery, website security or enterprise email operations;
  • where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, court order or lawful government request;
  • where necessary to protect our legal rights, website security, users or property;
  • in connection with a merger, restructuring, acquisition or transfer of the relevant business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements.

9. International Data Transfers

KFY SMART operates from China and serves business customers in different countries and regions.

Personal information may be processed in China and in other countries where our website-hosting, email-delivery, security and enterprise email service providers operate, which may include the United States.

Data-protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws in your jurisdiction. Where required by applicable law, we will use reasonable contractual, organizational or technical safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

10. Data Retention

For inquiries that do not result in an active business relationship, we generally retain the inquiry and related communication records for up to 24 months from the last meaningful communication.

We may retain information for a longer period where:

  • discussions or a business relationship remain active;
  • information is needed to complete an order, quotation or contractual process;
  • retention is required by applicable law;
  • information is necessary for accounting, tax, dispute-resolution, fraud-prevention or legal-claim purposes.

Spam, test submissions, clearly invalid inquiries and unnecessary records may be deleted earlier.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we will delete it or take reasonable steps to anonymize it.

11. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, organizational and technical measures designed to protect personal information, including:

  • limiting access to authorized personnel;
  • encrypted HTTPS transmission;
  • server-side form validation;
  • anti-spam and anti-bot protection when the inquiry form is enabled;
  • protected API keys and environment variables;
  • restricted access to website administration, hosting and email-delivery services;
  • reasonable monitoring of technical errors and suspicious activity.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We do not currently use:

  • Google Analytics 4;
  • Microsoft Clarity;
  • advertising pixels;
  • advertising retargeting tools;
  • non-essential marketing cookies.

The website and its service providers may use limited technical mechanisms necessary for:

  • website operation;
  • security;
  • inquiry-form submission when enabled;
  • spam and bot prevention;
  • infrastructure reliability.

If we introduce Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, advertising tools or other non-essential tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement any consent mechanism required by applicable law before or when those tools are enabled.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request information about how your personal information is processed;
  • request access to personal information held about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of information that is no longer required;
  • request restriction of certain processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request a portable copy of your information where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with an applicable data-protection authority.

To exercise a privacy right or submit a privacy-related question, contact:

privacy@kfygroup.com

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before processing a request. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

14. No Automated Decision-Making

We do not use website inquiry information to make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Website inquiries are reviewed and handled by authorized business personnel.

15. Children's Privacy

The KFY SMART website is intended for business and professional users and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

If we become aware that a minor has submitted personal information through our website, we will review the information and take reasonable steps to delete it where appropriate.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect:

  • changes to our website;
  • changes to our business processes;
  • the introduction of new service providers;
  • the introduction of analytics or security tools;
  • changes in applicable laws or regulatory requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page and the “Last Updated” date will be revised.

Where appropriate, material changes may also be communicated through a prominent notice on the website.
